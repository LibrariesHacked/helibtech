In the blog James Booth, Acquisitions and Reading Lists Assistant in the OER Team, shares the results of a project by the University of Leeds Library, which has developed useful definitions of Open Access materials and Open Educational Resources for use at the institution. The blog discusses the context for this work, as well as sharing the final key definitions developed.

Earlier blog posts from other UK institutions active in the OER space are available [here](https://sites.google.com/umn.edu/oer-community-uk-ireland/blog?authuser=0).
